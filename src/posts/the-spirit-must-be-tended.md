There's a dangerous assumption that spiritual awareness is the same as spiritual safety. It isn't. Here's why.

---

<div class="thought-entry">
<span class="entry-label">Raw Thoughts</span>

**The Three States**

The people who are spiritually dormant get possessed. Those who are spiritually aware but not tending to the Holy Spirit also offer themselves up for some form of possession — but in this case clandestinely. They become a conduit.

They tend to operate in a state they will think is self-awakening, and this consciousness will also be channelled towards things tending away from the Father.

---

**The Enemy's Mission**

Remember: the sole responsibility of evil spirits is to either not make man know or believe in God, or make their knowledge of God ineffective.

---

**What the Spirit Is**

The spirit is always seeking expression because that is the actual man. It is not that it was limited on purpose here on earth.

Man was an archetype of God — *"Let us make man in our own image"* — made to exercise dominion. Perhaps I could say at a lower cadre, but still in the form and sample of God.

But somehow he subjected himself to falsehood and broke a law, and God had to bring a spiritual mystery from the archives to redeem him (Ephesians 1).

---

**Two Natures**

The nature of what is not God is that it looks for a chance to *dominate* that person. But what is of God requires patience but guarantees peace.

<div class="reflection-entry">
<span class="entry-label">Reflection</span>

**Awareness Is Not Enough**

There's a category of person who is spiritually awake — they sense the unseen world, they have discernment, they know something is real — but they do not tend the Holy Spirit within them. They mistake *being awake* for *being filled*.

This is the most dangerous state because it feels like enlightenment. But an empty vessel that is aware it's a vessel will still be filled by whatever is nearest. If the Holy Spirit is not occupying that awareness, something else will — and quietly.

---

**What "Tended" Actually Means**

This must be answered clearly: *tended by what? And tended toward what?*

- **Tended by** the Holy Spirit — He is the gardener of the human spirit.
- **Tended toward** the Holy Spirit — active orientation through prayer, Word, fellowship, communion.
- **Tended how** — the same way you tend a fire: you feed it, guard it, keep it from being smothered. It is not passive. It requires patience, daily attention.

Here is the critical distinction:

> *Spiritual awareness* = the faculty is awake, like a radio receiving signals.
> *Tending the Holy Spirit* = the faculty is **tuned to the right frequency** and actively maintained there.

Awareness without tuning means the radio picks up *any* signal — including the wrong one. The person mistakes signal strength (heightened awareness) for signal source (the Holy Spirit). This is how clandestine possession works: the person feels more "spiritual" but is being fed from the wrong source.

Tending is not a static state. You do not arrive at "tended" and stay there. Neglect is not neutral — neglect is reversion to the untended state.

---

**The Enemy's Strategy**

The evil spirit's objective is binary: 

1. Keep you from knowing God at all.
2. If you know God, make that knowledge *ineffective*.

Strategy 2 is far more common among those who are spiritually aware but untended. They have the knowledge but not the fellowship. They can discern but they cannot resist. Their awareness becomes a liability because it opens them to a more sophisticated occupancy.

---

**Why the Spirit Must Express**

The spirit is the real person — the part that returns to God at death (Ecclesiastes 12:7). It is *designed* for expression, for communion, for dominion. Before the fall, that expression found its home in God. After the fall, man became a vacuum — and vacuums get filled.

The redemption in Ephesians 1 isn't just a legal transaction. It's a restoration of the spirit's *rightful occupant*. The Holy Spirit doesn't dominate — He indwells. Domination requires force. Indwelling requires invitation.

---

**Two Natures**

> What is not God *dominates*.
> What is of God *indwells*.

Domination is impatient. It pushes, controls, occupies. Indwelling is patient — it waits for tending. But its guarantee is peace, not coercion.

If your spiritual life feels like you're being *driven* rather than *led* — if your awareness produces restlessness, not peace — it may be time to ask who is occupying that awakened space.
